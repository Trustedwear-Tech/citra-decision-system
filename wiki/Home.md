# Citra Decision System

Build a decision app, an API or an embedded card by describing it in plain
English — over your own data, in your own infrastructure — and have it get
better every time one of your people corrects it.

The [README](https://github.com/Trustedwear-Tech/citra-decision-system) is the
short version. This wiki is the detail.

## A note on where things live

The website answers *why*. This wiki answers *how*. The README proves it is
real and gets you running.

Internal planning documents stay in the repository under `docs/` and are
deliberately **not** published here — they record decisions in progress, not
guidance you should follow.
