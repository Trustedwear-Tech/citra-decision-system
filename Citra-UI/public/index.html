Key Capabilities

  • Self-Improving Decision Apps & Decision API — high-quality recommendations for complex, high-stakes decisions (insurance claims, bank loans, KYC), learned from your officers' judgment. Built by your business team in plain English; take the built-in UI or run it headless.
  • Decision Memory — a Permanent Company Asset — every recommendation, the human decision, the WHY, and the real outcome are recorded. Your senior officers' intellect and judgment are retained by the company forever, even after they move on.
  • Ontology-Defined Governance — a schema defines exactly what the AI can and cannot do. Every write is approved or auditable; never an ungoverned model action.
  • 40–70% Lower Cost of Operations — routine cases auto-processed within your policy gate; officers review only the calls that need human judgment.
  • Operations Workflow — high-volume batch & event pipelines built by IT in plain English: AI evaluates each item against your rules, scores, splits, and produces the report — screen 1,000 applicants down to 10, end to end.
  • Operations Chat — ask your operational systems anything and get grounded, cited answers. "How many loans did I approve today?"
  • Live Dashboards — decisions and KPIs in real time, on your data, under your governance.
  • Sovereign by Design — open-source models in a dedicated single-tenant private cloud. Zero third-party egress; your data never trains anyone's models. MCP-based governed access to SQL, Mongo, S3, and REST APIs — no copy, no ETL.
