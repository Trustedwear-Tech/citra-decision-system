Operations Analytics — Answer Complex Operational Questions in a Conversation

By Rohit, Founder of Citra AI · May 6, 2026 · 10 min read

Most operational questions that actually matter — "what is the impact of this new policy on revenue and customer satisfaction?", "how is the new welfare scheme affecting citizen outcomes across districts?", "are our employees more or less engaged after the return-to-office mandate?" — never get answered properly. Not because the data isn't there. The data is in 14 different systems: SQL warehouses, the ERP, Salesforce, HR systems, survey platforms, file shares, PDF policy documents. Answering well requires pulling all of it together, cross-referencing, controlling for confounds, and writing it up.

Citra Operations Analytics answers that question in a single conversation. You ask; a long-running agent plans the investigation and does deep search across every connected system over MCP — then returns a decision-ready, fully cited report. Your data, your models, inside your perimeter.

How It Fits Alongside Operations Chat

1

Operations Chat

Fast AI chat over your governed enterprise data and the web. Cited answers across departments in seconds — ideal for daily knowledge work and grounded Q&A.

2

Operations Analytics

A long-running, sandboxed agentic investigation across every system. It plans, queries, cross-references, and writes a decision-ready, cited report — in one conversation.

Operations Chat is a real-time tool. Operations Analytics is something different — it is an autonomous research process that plans its own investigation, works across many systems, and produces a finished report.

What Operations Analytics Actually Does

You ask one complex operational question. Behind the scenes, a long-running agent runs the full investigation:

STEP 1

Plan

Decompose the question into sub-questions, hypotheses, and required evidence.

STEP 2

Discover

Identify which connected systems have relevant data via MCP — SQL, ERP, sales, HR, files.

STEP 3

Fetch

Auto-generate queries, pull rows, parse PDFs, sample documents, scrape allowed sources.

STEP 4

Analyze

Run deep search — segment, correlate, control for confounds, test sub-hypotheses.

STEP 5

Synthesize

Pull findings into a structured narrative with charts, tables, and citations to source rows and documents.

STEP 6

Deliver

Decision-ready report — delivered in the conversation, with a dashboard view.

The whole loop runs autonomously. You can close your laptop. When you come back, the report is ready, every claim is cited, and every chart is reproducible.

The shift in mental model

Stop thinking about "asking AI a question" and start thinking about tasking an investigation. Operations Analytics is not a chatbot — it takes a brief, runs the investigation across your systems, and returns a cited report.

Real Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Policy Impact on Company Performance

Strategy · Operations

Did our new return-to-office policy actually improve performance?

"Analyze the impact of our hybrid-to-RTO policy change on team output, attrition, eNPS, and revenue per employee — segmented by function and tenure — for the 6 months before and after the policy."

What the agent does: Pulls HR data from Workday, attrition logs from the HRMS, eNPS survey results from a file share, output metrics (commits, tickets, deals closed) from Jira / Salesforce / GitLab, revenue per FTE from finance data in SQL. Controls for seasonality and tenure mix. Cites every metric back to source rows.

Outcome: A 12-page impact report with statistically segmented findings, produced in a single conversation.

Use Case 2 — Citizen Welfare Impact (Government)

Public Sector · Welfare Schemes

Is the new welfare scheme actually improving citizen outcomes?

"Measure the impact of the new direct-benefit transfer scheme on household income, school enrolment, and healthcare utilization across the 18 pilot districts vs control districts, over the last 4 quarters."

What the agent does: Runs queries on the disbursement SQL system, pulls school enrolment data from the education department's API, ingests health records from the public hospital data lake, parses 200+ field-officer PDFs for qualitative signal, runs differential analysis between pilot and control districts.

Outcome: A cabinet-ready impact brief with district-level findings, charts, and quotes from field reports — produced in a single conversation inside a dedicated sovereign deployment.

Use Case 3 — Employee Sentiment on a Policy Change

HR · Sentiment

Are employees actually happy with the new performance-review framework?

"Quantify employee sentiment on the new continuous-feedback PMS rolled out in January — by department, manager level, and tenure — using survey data, internal forum posts, manager 1:1 notes, and exit interview transcripts."

What the agent does: Reads survey CSVs, ingests internal forum exports, parses anonymized 1:1 note PDFs, runs sentiment + theme extraction, segments by cohort, surfaces manager-level outliers, cites verbatim quotes (with consent flags respected).

Outcome: A sentiment heatmap by department + 20 representative verbatims + recommended interventions, produced in a single conversation.

Use Case 4 — Sales & ERP Cross-System Investigation

Revenue · Operations

Why is gross margin in the southern region down 4 points QoQ?

"Identify the drivers of the 4-point gross-margin compression in the southern region last quarter — broken out by SKU mix, discounting behaviour, freight cost, and channel partner."

What the agent does: Joins data from SAP (cost), Salesforce (discount approvals and deal notes), the freight TMS (transport cost per shipment), and partner contracts from a SharePoint folder. Decomposes the margin walk and ranks drivers.

Outcome: A margin-walk waterfall + driver attribution + 3 actionable recommendations, produced in a single conversation.

Use Case 5 — Compliance & Risk Scan

Risk · Compliance

Where are we exposed to the new regulation that drops next quarter?

"Map our exposure to the new data-localization regulation — across vendors, contracts, data flows, and customer segments — and rank remediation by effort and risk."

What the agent does: Reads the regulation PDF, parses 1,400 vendor contracts from the contract management system, pulls data-flow inventory from the architecture wiki, cross-references customer regions from the CRM, ranks gaps.

Outcome: A prioritized remediation plan with named owners and estimated effort — produced in a single conversation inside the bank's dedicated Citra deployment.

Why It Works — The Architecture in One Paragraph

Operations Analytics is built on three Citra primitives. MCP & Dept Data Flow give the agent governed, scoped access to every enterprise system — SQL, ERP, sales, HR, file servers — without ever exfiltrating data. Long-running agentic orchestration lets a single conversation persist through a full investigation, with checkpointing, retries, and audit logging across every tool call. Deep search means the agent doesn't just answer the question — it generates and tests sub-hypotheses, controls for confounds, cross-checks sources, and rewrites its plan when evidence shifts. The whole stack runs inside your sovereign Citra deployment on open-source models — dedicated private cloud, fully owned.
