# Retail Credit Policy — Acme Bank & Insurance Ltd

**Document type:** Policy · **Owner:** Credit Risk, Central Operations
**Classification:** Internal · **Version:** 4.2 · **Effective:** 01 April 2026

> Synthetic policy written for the Acme Bank demo tenant. Modelled on how
> Indian retail lenders write credit policy; not a reproduction of any real
> bank's document or of RBI text.

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs sanction of retail credit across the five products Acme
Bank offers: **home loans, personal loans, auto loans, loan against property
(LAP) and business loans**. It applies to every sourcing channel — branch,
DSA, digital and bancassurance.

Nothing in this policy overrides statutory or regulatory requirement. Where
this document and a regulatory instruction differ, the regulatory instruction
prevails and the deviation must be reported to Credit Risk within 2 working
days.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Age and residency
- Applicant must be 21–60 years at sanction and not exceed 65 at maturity
  (70 for LAP where a co-applicant with independent income is on record).
- Resident Indian applicants only. NRI applications route to the NRI desk and
  are outside this policy.

### 2.2 Income floors (monthly, net)

| Product | Salaried | Self-employed / business |
|---|---|---|
| Home | ₹30,000 | ₹40,000 |
| Personal | ₹25,000 | ₹40,000 |
| Auto | ₹20,000 | ₹30,000 |
| LAP | ₹35,000 | ₹45,000 |
| Business | n/a | ₹50,000 |

### 2.3 Vintage
- Salaried: 6 months in the current job, 2 years total employment.
- Self-employed / business: 3 years of business continuity evidenced by
  registration, GST or filed returns.

## 3. Obligation and exposure caps

### 3.1 FOIR (fixed obligation to income ratio)

FOIR = (all existing EMIs + the proposed EMI) ÷ net monthly income.

| Net monthly income | Maximum FOIR |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹50,000 | 45% |
| ₹50,001 – ₹1,50,000 | 55% |
| Above ₹1,50,000 | 60% |

Applications above the cap require Credit Manager approval with a written
justification. Above cap **plus** a bureau score under 700 is a decline, not a
deviation.

### 3.2 LTV (loan to value), secured products

| Product | Maximum LTV |
|---|---|
| Home, ticket up to ₹30 lakh | 90% |
| Home, ₹30 lakh – ₹75 lakh | 80% |
| Home, above ₹75 lakh | 75% |
| Auto (new) | 85% |
| Auto (used, up to 3 years) | 70% |
| LAP (residential) | 65% |
| LAP (commercial) | 55% |

Valuation must be from an empanelled valuer and not older than 90 days.

## 4. Bureau assessment

- A bureau report is mandatory on every application. Reports older than 30
  days at decision must be re-pulled.
- Indicative bands:

| Score | Treatment |
|---|---|
| 750+ | Normal processing |
| 700–749 | Normal, pricing may be adjusted |
| 650–699 | Credit Manager review |
| Below 650 | Decline unless fully secured and approved by Credit Head |
| No score (new to credit) | Permitted for salaried with 6 months' salary credits; requires income proof and Credit Manager review |

- **Any written-off or settled account** in the last 36 months is a decline
  irrespective of score.
- More than 6 bureau enquiries in the preceding 6 months must be explained by
  the applicant in writing (credit-hunger indicator).
- Overdue balances at other lenders must be cleared before disbursement.

## 5. Document requirements

| Requirement | Salaried | Self-employed / business |
|---|---|---|
| Identity and address (KYC) | Yes | Yes |
| PAN | Yes | Yes |
| Income proof | Payslips (3 months) + bank statement (6 months) | ITR (2 years) + bank statement (12 months) |
| Employment / business proof | Employer letter or ID | Registration, GST or trade licence |
| Property papers | Home / LAP only | Home / LAP only |

Income proof handling is set out in the **Income Verification SOP**; this
policy states only which documents are required, not how they are examined.

## 6. Pricing

Rate of interest is set by the published rate card by product, tenure and
bureau band. Deviations of more than 50 basis points require Credit Head
approval. Processing fee is capped at 2% of sanctioned amount.

## 7. Sanction authority

| Authority | Ticket size |
|---|---|
| Credit Officer | Up to ₹10 lakh, within policy |
| Credit Manager | Up to ₹50 lakh, or any within-policy deviation |
| Regional Credit Head | Up to ₹2 crore |
| Credit Committee | Above ₹2 crore, and every policy exception |

An officer must not sanction a case they sourced.

## 8. Decline reasons

Every decline must be recorded against one of: income below floor, FOIR above
cap, bureau adverse, document deficiency, property or valuation issue,
employment or business vintage, negative area or profile, or applicant
withdrawal. Free-text reasons alone are not acceptable — the coded reason is
what lets Credit Risk see patterns across a portfolio.

## 9. Review

Credit Risk reviews this policy annually, and immediately upon a regulatory
change affecting eligibility, exposure or classification.
