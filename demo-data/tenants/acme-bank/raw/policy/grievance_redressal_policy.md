# Grievance Redressal Policy — Acme Bank & Insurance Ltd

**Document type:** Policy · **Owner:** Customer Service, Central Operations
**Classification:** Public · **Version:** 3.0 · **Effective:** 01 April 2026

> Synthetic policy written for the Acme Bank demo tenant.

---

## 1. Principle

A complaint is information about a failure in our service. It is recorded,
answered and used — not deflected. Every customer-facing officer may register a
complaint on the customer's behalf; no customer is asked to write to a
different address to be heard.

## 2. How a complaint may be made

Branch, call centre, website form, email, post, or through a relationship
manager. Every complaint receives a **complaint reference number** at the point
of registration, regardless of channel.

## 3. Escalation ladder

| Level | Who | Respond within |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Branch Manager / Customer Service Officer | 7 working days |
| 2 | Regional Nodal Officer | 10 working days |
| 3 | Principal Nodal Officer | 15 working days |
| 4 | External ombudsman scheme | Per scheme rules |

A customer may escalate when a level has not responded within its timeline, or
when the response does not resolve the matter. The customer is **told the next
level** in every response — a response that does not state the escalation route
is incomplete.

## 4. Response standards

A response states: what was complained about, what was found, what will be done
(or why nothing will be), and by when. Responses are in the language of the
original complaint where that is English, Hindi or the branch's regional
language.

Where a complaint is upheld, remediation is completed before the response is
sent wherever possible, and the customer is told what has changed.

## 5. Categories

Complaints are categorised at registration, because the category is what makes
patterns visible:

- service delay
- incorrect charge or interest
- mis-selling
- recovery conduct
- claim decision
- data or privacy
- staff behaviour
- digital or technical failure

Miscategorisation is corrected on review; a large "other" share is treated as a
defect in this list, not in the customers.

## 6. Recovery-conduct complaints

Any complaint alleging a breach of the recovery conduct rules in the **Fair
Practices Code** is:

- registered as `recovery conduct` irrespective of how it arrives,
- routed to the Collections Head **and** Compliance on the same day,
- accompanied by suspension of field activity on that account pending review.

## 7. Claim-decision complaints

A complaint about a repudiated or partly settled claim is reviewed by an officer
who did **not** decide the original claim. Where the original decision is
reversed, the reason is recorded against the original claim as well, so the
pattern is visible to the claims team.

## 8. Reporting

| Report | Frequency | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| Complaint volumes by category and ageing | Monthly | Business Heads |
| Level-2 and above escalations | Monthly | Principal Nodal Officer |
| Upheld complaints and remediation | Quarterly | Board Customer Service Committee |
| Root-cause themes | Quarterly | Process owners |

## 9. Non-retaliation

No customer is disadvantaged in any service, pricing or decision because they
complained. Any suggestion otherwise is itself a reportable incident.
