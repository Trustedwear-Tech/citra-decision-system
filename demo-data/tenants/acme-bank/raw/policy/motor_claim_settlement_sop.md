# Motor Claim Settlement SOP — Acme Bank & Insurance Ltd

**Document type:** SOP · **Owner:** Claims, General Insurance
**Classification:** Internal · **Version:** 4.3 · **Effective:** 01 April 2026

> Synthetic SOP written for the Acme Bank demo tenant. Modelled on Indian
> general-insurance claims practice; not a reproduction of IRDAI text.

---

## 1. Scope

Own damage, third party and theft claims under motor policies issued by Acme
Bank & Insurance.

## 2. Intimation

- A claim must be intimated within the **`intimation_window_days` stated on the
  policy** (commonly 30 days, 15 on some products, 45 on others). The window is
  measured from the **date of loss**, not from the date of repair or discovery.
- Intimation may be by any channel; the claim is created with the date and time
  of first intimation regardless of channel.
- **Theft claims additionally require an FIR.** A theft claim without an FIR
  number cannot proceed past intimation.

### 2.1 Late intimation

A claim intimated beyond the policy window is **not automatically repudiated**.
The officer records the delay, obtains the reason in writing, and refers it:

| Delay beyond window | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Up to 7 days | Officer may condone with reason recorded |
| 8–30 days | Claims Manager approval required |
| More than 30 days | Repudiation unless the reason is compelling and documented (hospitalisation of the insured, custody of the vehicle with police, natural calamity) |

Delay alone is never the sole recorded reason where the loss itself is not in
doubt — state the full basis.

## 3. Documents

| Claim type | Mandatory |
|---|---|
| All | Policy copy, claim form, driving licence, registration certificate |
| Own damage | Repair estimate, damage photographs |
| Theft | FIR, non-traceable report (when issued), both keys, RTO transfer papers |
| Third party | FIR, details of the third party and their claim |

Damage photographs must show the vehicle, the registration plate and the
damaged area in the same frame where physically possible.

## 4. Surveyor appointment

| Estimated loss | Action |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹50,000 | Officer may settle without survey, on photographs and estimate |
| ₹50,001 – ₹5,00,000 | Survey by an empanelled surveyor within 3 working days |
| Above ₹5,00,000 | Survey plus Claims Manager review |
| Theft, or any claim with a fraud indicator | Survey plus investigation, irrespective of amount |

The surveyor's assessed amount, not the estimate, is the basis for settlement.
Where the officer settles above the assessed amount, the reason is recorded.

## 5. Assessment

1. Verify the policy was **in force on the date of loss** and the premium was
   paid.
2. Verify the driver held a valid licence for the class of vehicle.
3. Apply depreciation on parts per the policy schedule.
4. Apply the compulsory deductible and any voluntary excess.
5. Deduct salvage value where the insured retains the salvage.
6. Check for **prior claims** on the same policy and the same damage area.

## 6. Exclusions

Common grounds for repudiation, each of which must be evidenced, not assumed:

- driving under the influence, or without a valid licence;
- use of a private vehicle for hire or reward;
- consequential loss, wear and tear, mechanical breakdown;
- loss outside the geographical area of the policy;
- deliberate act by the insured;
- policy not in force on the date of loss.

## 7. Turnaround

| Stage | TAT |
|---|---|
| Surveyor appointed | 3 working days from intimation |
| Survey report received | 7 working days from appointment |
| Decision after complete documents | 7 working days |
| Payment after approval | 5 working days |

## 8. Repudiation

A repudiation letter states the specific policy clause relied on, the facts
found, and the customer's right to escalate under the **Grievance Redressal
Policy**. A repudiation recorded only as "documents insufficient" is not
acceptable — state what was insufficient and what was sought.

## 9. Cashless versus reimbursement

- Cashless is available at network garages; the garage raises the estimate and
  the surveyor assesses before work begins.
- Reimbursement requires the original invoice and payment proof.
- The customer chooses; the officer does not steer the customer to a garage in
  which any employee has an interest.
