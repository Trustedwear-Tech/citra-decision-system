# Sales Conduct and Suitability — Acme Bank & Insurance Ltd

**Document type:** Code of Conduct · **Owner:** Distribution, with Compliance
**Classification:** Internal · **Version:** 2.2 · **Effective:** 01 April 2026

> Synthetic code written for the Acme Bank demo tenant.

---

## 1. Principle

We sell a customer the product they need on terms they understand. A sale that
meets a target but not a need is a loss — it returns as a complaint, a lapse,
a repudiated claim or a delinquency, and it costs more than the sale earned.

## 2. Suitability

Before recommending any credit or insurance product, the seller establishes and
records:

- the customer's stated need and time horizon,
- income and existing obligations,
- existing cover or borrowing with us and elsewhere,
- for insurance: dependants, and any material health or occupation factor.

A recommendation outside the customer's stated need requires the customer's
written acknowledgement of why.

## 3. Disclosure

At or before the point of sale, in the customer's language, state:

| Product | Must be disclosed |
|---|---|
| Any loan | Rate and whether fixed or floating, all fees, EMI, tenure, penal charges, prepayment terms |
| Any insurance | Premium and payment term, sum insured, **waiting periods and exclusions**, free-look period, lapse consequences |
| Bancassurance | That the insurance is a separate product from the loan, and is **optional** |

**Never** state or imply that:

- a loan approval depends on buying insurance,
- an insurance policy is a deposit or an investment guarantee,
- returns are assured, where they are not,
- a waiting period does not apply, when it does.

## 4. Prohibited practices

- Signing, or asking the customer to sign, an incomplete form.
- Completing a need-analysis after the sale to fit the product sold.
- Splitting one requirement into multiple policies to increase commission.
- Replacing an existing policy without documenting why the replacement is better
  for the customer.
- Selling to a customer who cannot understand the terms without an independent
  witness present and recorded.

## 5. Bancassurance specifics

- The seller holds a valid IRDAI licence, recorded against `agents.licence_no`.
- The customer is told the insurer's name — the product is not presented as a
  bank deposit product.
- Free-look rights are explained, and the free-look window is stated in writing.

## 6. Lead handling

- Leads are assigned to a licensed, active agent. An unassigned lead ageing
  beyond **3 working days** is a service failure and is escalated to the Branch
  Sales Manager.
- Customer contact details from a lead are used for that lead only, and are not
  retained after the lead is closed as lost.
- Contact hours follow the Fair Practices Code: **08:00 to 19:00**.

## 7. Incentives

Incentives are structured on **net** business after cancellations, free-look
returns and early lapses, so that a poor sale does not pay. Persistency and
complaint rates form part of every seller's assessment alongside volume.

## 8. Monitoring

| Check | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Need-analysis completeness sampling | Monthly, 5% of sales |
| Free-look cancellation analysis by seller | Monthly |
| Early lapse (within 12 months) by seller | Quarterly |
| Mis-selling complaints by seller and branch | Monthly |

Three substantiated mis-selling complaints in 12 months trigger a review of the
seller's authorisation.
