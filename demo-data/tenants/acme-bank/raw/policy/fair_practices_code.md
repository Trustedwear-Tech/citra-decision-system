# Fair Practices Code — Acme Bank & Insurance Ltd

**Document type:** Code of Conduct · **Owner:** Compliance
**Classification:** Public · **Version:** 2.4 · **Effective:** 01 April 2026

> Synthetic code written for the Acme Bank demo tenant. Modelled on the Fair
> Practices Code Indian lenders publish; not a reproduction of any real text.

---

## 1. Commitment

Acme Bank commits to dealing with customers fairly, transparently and without
discrimination on the basis of gender, religion, caste, disability or marital
status. This Code is published on our website and available at every branch in
English and the regional language.

## 2. Applications and processing

- Every loan application is acknowledged with a reference number and an
  indicative turnaround time.
- The applicant is told, up front, what documents are required and what fees
  apply.
- A decision is communicated in writing. **Where an application is rejected,
  the communication states the principal reason.**
- Turnaround commitments:

| Product | Decision TAT |
|---|---|
| Personal, auto | 3 working days from complete file |
| Home, LAP | 10 working days from complete file |
| Business | 12 working days from complete file |

An incomplete file is not a decision TAT breach, but the deficiency must be
communicated within 2 working days.

## 3. Sanction and terms

The sanction letter states, in plain language: amount, rate of interest and
whether it is fixed or floating, tenure, EMI, all fees and charges, the
penalty for late payment, prepayment and foreclosure terms, and the security
taken. The customer's acknowledgement of the sanction letter is retained.

Any change in terms — rate, fee, charge — is notified before it takes effect,
and is applied prospectively only.

## 4. Interest and charges

- Rates are set by the published rate card and are not varied by the individual
  officer.
- Penal charges for delayed payment are levied on the overdue amount, not on
  the whole outstanding, and are disclosed at sanction.
- Fees are not levied for services the customer did not ask for.

## 5. Recovery conduct

Recovery is the recovery of a debt, not a punishment. Acme Bank and every agent
acting for it will:

- contact the customer between **08:00 and 19:00** only;
- identify themselves, and state on whose behalf they are calling;
- not use threatening or abusive language, or any form of intimidation;
- not contact the customer's employer, relatives or neighbours to apply
  pressure, and not disclose the debt to any third party;
- not visit at a time or place the customer has reasonably objected to;
- leave immediately when asked to, at a residence or workplace;
- treat a customer in genuine hardship with a view to a workable arrangement.

A breach of this section is a disciplinary matter and, for an agency, grounds
for termination of the engagement.

## 6. Privacy

Customer information is used only for the purpose for which it was collected
and is not shared with a third party without consent, except where disclosure
is required by law or by a regulator.

## 7. Grievance redressal

A customer who is dissatisfied may escalate as set out in the **Grievance
Redressal Policy**. The escalation ladder and the contact details of the
Principal Nodal Officer are displayed at every branch and on the website.

## 8. Language and accessibility

Customers may transact in English, Hindi or the regional language of the
branch. Branch staff assist customers who cannot read a document, and record
that assistance was given.
